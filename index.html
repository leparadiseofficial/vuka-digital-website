Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a website cost?

Our website packages start from R1,500. The final price depends on the number of pages, features and requirements.

How long does a website take?

Simple business websites can often be completed within a few days, depending on the content, requirements and requested changes.

Will my website work on a phone?

Yes. Our websites are designed to be responsive and usable on phones, tablets and desktop computers.

Can I contact Vuka Digital on WhatsApp?

Yes. WhatsApp is one of the easiest ways to contact us about a website project.

Can you update my website later?

Yes. We can help with website updates, changes and maintenance after your website has been published.

Do I need to know coding?

No. You simply tell us what your business needs and we handle the website development.
